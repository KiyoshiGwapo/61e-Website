Hit 10 moving infantry targets in five minutes. A musket fires once, then you must go through the full loading drill (about 7 seconds). Make the shot count. On Hard, a hussar on horseback zigzags toward you; he dodges when your sights are on him, so time your shot. Move with WASD.
